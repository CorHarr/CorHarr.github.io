## About

I am a data analyst completing my M.S. in Data Science at Bellevue University, with a goal of supporting decision-making in the federal government. My work focuses on taking large, messy datasets, often from public agencies, and turning them into findings that leaders can act on: where to direct resources, when to adjust staffing, and which problems to address first.

**What I bring**

- **Data cleaning and preparation:** standardizing codes, parsing dates, fixing data types, and combining multiple sources into analysis-ready data
- **Attention to detail:** careful checks so findings hold up to scrutiny
- **Creative problem solving:** looking past the obvious metric to find the more useful question
- **Independent and collaborative:** comfortable owning a project from start to finish and contributing as part of a team

**Tools:** SQL · Python (pandas, scikit-learn, Plotly) · R · Excel · Power BI

## Projects

### Public Sector & Operations

**TSA Complaint Hotspots** (Python)
Analyzed about one million TSA complaint records by airport, category, and month to identify where training and resources would have the greatest impact. *Code and full write-up coming soon.*

**White House Visitor Traffic Analysis** (Python, Plotly)
Analyzed about 303,000 visitor records with interactive visualizations to identify traffic patterns and recommend staffing for peak periods. *Code and full write-up coming soon.*

### Predictive Modeling
